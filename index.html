Common questions

Before you ask.

What is a Better Way Discovery Session?

A ninety-minute conversation about one problem that's been bothering you. Ariel asks questions, you do most of the talking, and together you figure out what's actually going wrong rather than what it looks like from the outside.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no proposal at the end unless you ask for one.

Do I need to know what my problem is first?

No. "Something about this feels harder than it should" is a perfectly good starting point, and it's usually a more honest one than a tidy problem statement.

Are you going to tell me I need AI?

Maybe not. Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes it's a process nobody has questioned in years, or better communication, or training, or software you already own. Sometimes nothing new needs to be bought at all.

What do I leave with?

A clearer picture of what's actually broken, and at least one thing you can act on that week. If you want to go deeper afterward, there's a paid Better Way Assessment — a fuller look at how your organization works today and where the meaningful improvements are.

Who is this for?

Leaders and teams who know something isn't working but are too close to see what it is. Ariel Builds works with organizations of most sizes, not just startups.

Where are you based?

Austin, Texas. Sessions happen over video, so where you are isn't a constraint.
